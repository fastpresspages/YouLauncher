ABOUT

A small growth studio for people who ship.

YouLauncher is built in public by Meet Shah as part of FastPress Labs.

What this is

YouLauncher helps people move from an idea to a test, from a business to a working engine, or from a product to a launch week. The unit of work is a clear next move, not a 60-slide deck.

How the work happens

  • You describe the stage. Idea, business or product. A link and one paragraph is enough to start.
  • AI assists with the drafts. Research, positioning options, copy, checklists and launch assets can be drafted quickly.
  • A human reviews before it ships. Nothing is sent to you — or published for you — without a person deciding it is good enough.
  • You keep the final call. The plan is a recommendation. You decide what to build, publish and charge.

What we will not do

  • Invent customers, revenue, testimonials or case studies.
  • Promise a specific number of signups, followers or dollars.
  • Send you a fake “personal” email from a bot and call it human.
  • Sell your email or add you to a list you did not ask for.

Why the site is honest about being early

YouLauncher does not have a wall of logos yet. What it has is a process, a public repo, and a free audit you can run yourself. If a result is published later, it will have a date, a source and permission.

Free page audit

The launch page itself is graded by LaunchGrade, a free open-source tool from the same studio. You can run it on your own page, or ask for a human read through the collab form.
