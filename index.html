Questions people actually ask.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The first read of your idea, business or product page is free. Paid builds and launch sprints are scoped separately and agreed in writing before work starts.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within two business days. A human reads every request. If the queue is longer, the reply will say so instead of pretending a bot is a person.

Do you use AI?

AI assists with research, first drafts, copy options and checklists. A human reviews deliverables before they are sent. AI does not publish or contact anyone on its own.

Do you guarantee signups or revenue?

No. No one can honestly guarantee signups, revenue, funding or press. YouLauncher publishes process, not invented case studies, and any future result will include a date, a source and permission.
